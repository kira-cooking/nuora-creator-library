start here

He never tells her she has a problem

Look at how he starts every line. He says "if you’re scared of". He does not say "you have this problem".

That is a small change and it does a lot. Being scared is a feeling she already has. Nobody has to tell her about it. So there is nothing to argue with, and she just nods along.

  • 0:00"If you’re scared of that Ozempic booty, you gotta take creatine."
  • 0:04"Scared of your hair thinning and falling out? You have to take collagen."
  • 0:08"Scared of that ugly, droopy, saggy skin? Hyaluronic acid, trust me."
  • 0:12"Or maybe you hit a stall. Vitamin D3 is a must for you."

Now count what just happened. Four fears. And every single one comes with one more bottle she would have to go buy.

She adds it up in her own head. Four bottles, every day, for months. Nobody told her it was too much. She worked that out by herself, which is why she believes it.

so

Say "if you’re scared of" instead of "you have". Let her agree with a feeling instead of admitting a problem.

the trick

He prints the photos and holds them up

The photos are printed on paper. He holds them next to his head and points at them with a red marker. There is no editing here at all.

And the photos do the hardest part for him. He never has to say the ugly words out loud, because she can already see it. He gets to stay friendly the whole time.

so

Print your pictures. Hold them up. Point at them. Your phone and a marker is the whole setup.

the line that does the work

Then he says the cost out loud

“I know how overwhelming and how pricey that can be, to take all of those separately every single day, for month after month.”

He waited until 18 seconds to say this. By then she has already built the pile in her head, so he is only naming what she is feeling.

And notice what he is really offering her. He is not selling a tub. He is taking four worries off her at once. That is the part she actually wants.

The tub shows up at 25 seconds, which is halfway through. Everything before that is her fears and her money. Nothing before that is about him.

so

Build the pile first. Then say out loud how much it costs and how tiring it is. Then bring your product in.

two rules

Before you film

please keep these two

Do not build your video on a weight loss drug. Jake does and it works for him, but we cannot go near that. Use her own fears about her own body and it works just as well.

Never promise what will happen to her. Say what happened for you. "Helps support" is the phrase when you talk about what it does. That one word keeps your account safe.

Everything else is yours. Your fears, your words, your face. If it sounds like you talking to a friend, it is right.
