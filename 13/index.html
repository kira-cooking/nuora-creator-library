The one doing nothing is the one you want to be

Watch only the man on the right. He does not lean in. He does not defend his tub. He answers without putting his glass down, and twice he cannot even be bothered to finish the sentence properly.

Five separate times, the other man introduces an ingredient with a dose and a reason. Five separate times, the answer is the same, and he never once improves it.

the one people get wrong

the part you actually need

Filming a two-person video on your own

Watch the first minute again and count how many times they touch each other’s side of the table. It never happens. They sit in separate frames, they never pass a bottle across, and the only contact is their voices.

Which means this was never really a two person video. It is two performances placed next to each other, and one person can shoot both. Three ways to do it, easiest last.

Three ways, all solo

Pick whichever suits the day you are having. All three produce the same effect on the woman watching.

option 1

Two chairs, one camera

what it does
You play both sides. The camera stays locked, you film one seat, then move over and film the other.
why it works
It reproduces the original exactly, because the original was already two separate recordings. We have this in the library already: one creator, two chairs, a nurse and a patient, no budget and no second person.
making it yours
Change one visible detail between takes so the two read as different people. Film the loaded side of the table first while everything is out, then clear it and film the calm side.
option 2

Before and now

what it does
Your old routine is the pile. Your routine now is the answer. No acting and no second character.
why it works
You get the same contrast, and every word of it is your own experience, which is the most believable register you own and the safest one. The woman watching has a version of that pile in her own cupboard, so she is counting along with you.
making it yours
Physically set out what you used to buy. Pick each one up, say what you bought it for, put it down. Then answer yourself with the same short phrase every single time.
option 3

One hand, one take

what it does
The product stays in one hand for the whole video. Your other hand picks up each old bottle, names it, and sets it down.
why it works
One take, one camera position, no second setup. The repeating on your part carries it, so a shaky first attempt still works and you can film it in the ten minutes before you leave the house.
making it yours
Keep your answering phrase identical every time. Dressing it up on the third round is what breaks it, because she stops being able to see it coming.
the one rule that carries all three

He says the same five words five times and never once improves them. By the third round the viewer is saying it with him, and a woman who is finishing your sentences has already picked her side of the table.

Say it flat, say it identically, and resist making it funnier each time.
