Your delivery is the difference.

Open with recognitionA moment she can picture.

Say the problem plainly: bloating that makes her clothes feel tight, or intimate smell she worries someone else might notice. Then describe a moment she recognizes.

Earn trust through clarityOne point. Show it.

Use the bottle and label like you used the ball and ring. Explain one approved product fact with a clear close-up.

Invite her to look at the product details. Your normal clothes, natural setting and conversational delivery are enough.

From a man’s perspective: The aim is “He understands what this is like.” Speak with her, not about what a man expects from her. If your partner has struggled with bloating or intimate odor, you can use her experience as an example.
